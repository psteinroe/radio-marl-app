# Radio Marl App

My uncles biggest passion is radion, and he is running his own little station in his hometown. He approached me how costly it would be to have an app for his station. I told him that I can build him one for free, and that I would be happy to do so. This is the result of that promise. Made possible thanks to the generous free tier of Expo.

Please check out his station at [Radio Marl](https://marl-radio.de). You would make him very happy. And me too. :)
